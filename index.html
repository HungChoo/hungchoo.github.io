About me

People first, process always.

I’m a recruiter and People professional at Bengal Group of Industries, one of Bangladesh’s diversified business groups. My work sits where talent acquisition meets people operations: sourcing hard-to-find technical talent, running structured hiring, and supporting the employee journey from offer to onboarding.

I believe good hiring is a partnership. I take time to understand the business, the team and the role, then headhunt candidates who fit the work and the culture.
